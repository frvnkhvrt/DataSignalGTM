# DataSignalGTM — Demo Script

## Pitch Day: May 10 → May 11

---

## Overview

DataSignalGTM is a GTM intelligence dashboard that ingests buying signals from multiple sources (LinkedIn hiring, funding news, intent signals, CRM migrations, job postings) and uses AI (Gemini) to auto-generate personalized playbooks for each account. Data quality gates prevent reps from wasting cycles on incomplete records.

**Stack:** Supabase (Postgres) · Lovable (React frontend) · FastAPI + Gemini (AI playbooks) · Make (automation)

## If They Ask...

**Q: How do signals get ingested?**

A: Make webhooks pick up triggers from LinkedIn Sales Nav, Apollo, and CRM exports. FastAPI normalizes and scores them.

**Q: What's the AI model?**

A: Google Gemini via FastAPI. The prompt includes the account's signal type, company context, and ICP fit score.

**Q: How do you prevent hallucinations?**

A: Playbooks are structured JSON with fixed fields. Gemini fills in the values within the template constraints.

**Q: What's the GTM Health Score?**

A: Live average of all account DQ scores. Goes up as reps fix data issues.
